# Where to look

The [source catalogue](catalog.json) is a set of starting leads, not a list to sweep. For the method, see [finding jobs](../docs/finding-jobs.md).

During setup, [plan where to look](../workflows/find-jobs.md#plan-where-to-look): choose three to six sources that fit the person's country, language, profession and level, and save them in `research/source-plan.md`. Always include their target companies' job boards or careers pages. Don't assume an English-speaking office job or a US or UK location.

| Need | Starting points |
|---|---|
| Company job boards (where to apply) | Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, Recruitee, read with `ats_search.py` |
| Finding companies | [LinkedIn Jobs](https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/), [Indeed](https://www.indeed.com/), [Hacker News "Who is hiring?"](https://news.ycombinator.com/submitted?id=whoishiring) |
| Startups | [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/jobs), [Work at a Startup](https://www.workatastartup.com/), [Welcome to the Jungle](https://www.welcometothejungle.com/), [Built In](https://builtin.com/jobs) (US) |
| Public employment services | [UK Find a job](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-job), [USAJOBS](https://www.usajobs.gov/), [Canada Job Bank](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/), [EURES](https://eures.europa.eu/) |
| Regional | [SEEK Australia](https://au.seek.com/), and local alternatives you research |
| Climate and nonprofit | [Climatebase](https://climatebase.org/), [Idealist](https://www.idealist.org/) |
| Remote | [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/); check which countries each job allows |
| Teaching in England | [Teaching Vacancies](https://teaching-vacancies.service.gov.uk/) |

`verification` in the catalogue records only whether the homepage opened on `last_checked`: `homepage_reachable`, `unverified_tool_error` (the check failed, which may just be bot blocking) or `not_checked`. It says nothing about live vacancies. Healthcare, academic and some regional entries are unverified. Recheck them before recommending.

Don't create accounts, pay for features or get around access controls. Ask the person first. Revisit sources when results are poor or their direction changes.
