# Stories

Specific examples the assistant uses in cover letters, application answers and interviews. Aim for 8–12, covering:

- a big achievement;
- leading or influencing people;
- a failure and what they learned;
- a disagreement handled well;
- dealing with ambiguity;
- their core skill at its best.

Rank each one: **A** = strong, specific and measurable; **B** = good but missing a number or detail; **C** = usable but thin. Ask follow-up questions to turn a B into an A. Estimates are fine if labelled as estimates. Keep what they did separate from what the team did.

Format:

```md
## Short title (rank A/B/C)
- Role and dates:
- Situation:
- Task: what they were responsible for
- Action: what they personally did
- Result: what changed, with numbers where known
- Learned:
- Good for: themes and question types (for example leadership, conflict, "tell me about a time you failed")
- Source: CV, review, or "told in chat YYYY-MM-DD"
```
