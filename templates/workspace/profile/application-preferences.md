# How applications are handled

Suggested defaults. Confirm them with the person during setup, and record any change with the date.

- **Review:** show the tailored CV, cover letter and every answer together, plus any decisions only they can make.
- **Approval:** nothing is submitted until they've seen the package and asked for it to be sent. One message can approve a whole batch.
- **Salary:** follow the disclosure choice in [answers.md](answers.md). If a form requires a number and no choice is recorded, stop and ask. Never pick one from a range or from research.
- **Marketing or future-opportunities emails:** decline.
- **Required privacy consent:** accept when needed to apply, once they've confirmed this standing choice.
- **Email:** manual unless `workspace.json` says otherwise. This covers reading email in the browser too.
- **Outreach, public posts and work samples:** only with a separate instruction each time.
