# Applying in bulk

You don't have to apply one job at a time. Nox-Lite is built to find, prepare and submit a whole batch in one conversation, with one review and one approval.

## A typical day

**You:** "Find me 20–25 new jobs."

The assistant searches employers' job boards and the other sources in your plan, checks each job is real and still open, removes duplicates and jobs you've already seen, and scores the rest strictly. You get a numbered list: company, role, location, salary if shown, a fit score and the main reason. If only 12 good jobs exist today, it says so instead of padding the list.

**You:** "Prepare 1–15, except 7."

Each job gets its own tailored CV, a cover letter where the form allows one, and an answer for every question on the form. Your standard answers (right to work, salary, notice period and so on) are reused automatically. New questions come back to you once, grouped: *"Three questions cover 11 of these jobs; two are employer-specific, with my suggestions."*

**Assistant:** "14 applications are ready to review."

You get one numbered review linking every CV, letter and answer, with anything that needs your decision highlighted. Say things like "Shorter cover letter for number 4", "Show me the CV for 12" or "Hold 9".

**You:** "Approve and submit all of them except 9."

That one message is enough. The assistant submits each application in your Chrome, checks for real confirmation, and records the result in your tracker. It doesn't stop to ask permission between jobs.

## When something needs you

- **A login, CAPTCHA, verification code or online test:** it tells you exactly what's needed and carries on with the other applications meanwhile.
- **A new question on the form:** it asks you about that job and submits the rest.
- **No clear confirmation:** that job is marked unconfirmed and checked, never submitted twice.
- **A usage limit:** everything is saved. When it resets, say "Continue my batch."

At the end you get a summary, for example "12 submitted; 2 need a verification code from you", and an offer to find the next batch.

## How many

Start with 5 jobs so the assistant can learn your taste. After that, 20–25 per search is a good rhythm. Say "Find 40 today" for a one-off, or "Find 15 per search from now on" to change your usual number. On a Free or entry-level plan, preparing about ten at a time avoids running out of allowance mid-batch.

To search automatically each morning, see [schedules](../setup/schedules.md). A schedule only finds jobs. You still choose and approve.
